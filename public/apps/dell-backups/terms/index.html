Dell Trading Backups

Terms of use

Effective October 29, 2026 · Maintained by Joe Armani

Personal use

Dell Trading Backups is a personal backup utility maintained by Joe Armani for his own Windows workstation and authorized Google Drive account. Access to another person’s computer, files, or account requires that person’s authorization.

Configuration and account responsibility

The operator chooses the folders to preserve, grants Google account access, and is responsible for available local and cloud storage. A successful backup covers the configured scope. The operator should inspect the recorded verification result before relying on an archive.

Verification and recovery

The utility checks downloaded file hashes and SQLite database integrity. Those checks establish that the archived files can be restored. They do not establish that a running application or trading session can resume safely from those files. Recovery into operational directories requires a separate decision by the operator.

Backups can fail because of account restrictions, storage limits, network availability, changing source files, or other errors. A setup or scheduling action alone does not establish that a backup completed. The utility records completed verification separately from a failed or pending operation.

Data and Google services

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. Google account and Drive use remain subject to Google’s applicable terms. The account owner controls consent, retained archives, and removal of account access.
